The Horror Nights

On a dark night, a white Hengtons private jet was flying in the cloudy sky above Trimpton Forest. People believe that strange animals and creatures live there. Just when the ride was going smooth, suddenly, the jet’s loud alarm went off. The plane was crashing! Pilots and the Hengtons couldn't survive, except for Linda Hengton, the youngest member of the Hengton Family. Unfortunately young Linda was crying of the death of her mom, dad, uncle, aunty, and sadly her loved grandpa too. 1
When Linda fell out of her parachute, she blinked, looked around, and felt unconscious. When she opened her eyes, there was a big house in front of her. She tried to go inside but the door was locked. When she came at the back the door was open. The door creaked open, and she went inside — it was all dark with spooky items. The house looked abandoned. She went upstairs and entered a room. A spooky white flash swooshed behind her. Just when she turned to look behind, it disappeared! She was enormously frightened. At that moment, the door shut behind her. Although she was scared, she tried to open the door but regrettably it was locked. She tried to open the window — luckily, the window was unlocked. She jumped on a pile of leaves but just at that time she heard a soft spooky sound coming from the back. She hid in the leaves and waited for the sound to come. It was a ghost named Shadow. He was one of the dangerous ghosts on earth.
